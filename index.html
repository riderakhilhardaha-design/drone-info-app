Drone License / Certificate

In India, requirements depend on drone category and operation. Check current DGCA/Digital Sky rules and use an authorised training organisation where required.

Typical training path

01
Check eligibility

Confirm age, education and other requirements with the training provider.

02
Choose an authorised RPTO

Verify the organisation through official DGCA resources.

03
Complete theory and practical training

Study regulations, weather, safety and practical flight skills.

04
Follow the official certification process

Complete required assessments and documentation.

This is general information, not legal advice. Confirm current requirements with DGCA and an authorised training provider.
